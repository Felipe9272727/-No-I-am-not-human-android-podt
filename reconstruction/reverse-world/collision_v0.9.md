# Collision correction v0.9

The Android renderer treats the player as three vertical sphere samples with radius 0.28 m. BoxCollider contact now transforms the closest local-space point back through the collider's world linear transform, then compares the true world-space distance against that radius. It no longer inflates every local axis by a single inverse-scale maximum, which made narrow, highly anisotropic colliders block open space.

Active box transforms in this exported scene have orthogonal axes (QA allows normalized axis dot products below 1e-5). The exported Unity mesh triangles remain the source for ordinary floor support. Box support precomputes the most upward-facing OBB face and its XZ bounds when the collider is built, including a face whose local Z axis points upward. No active solid source box has two axes above the normalized 0.55 upward threshold. Thin tilted hatches can then participate in the existing 0.32 m step-up and 1.25 m step-down window without allocating during floor queries. Those tolerances belong to this port adapter; they are not recovered ECM2 movement settings. The player's 0.28 m footprint uses a closest-point check against all four face edges; collision and the existing mesh checks still govern walls and headroom.

`tools/android-current/qa/WorldGeometryTest.java` includes OBB checks for near side contact, a clear 0.5 m offset, source hatch 2407 support, two-direction stepped movement over source hatch 2407, step-height limits, headroom, and orthogonality of active source box axes. The broader scene movement check continues to verify the source entrance wall and ray targets. This is host-side geometry QA, not evidence of Android runtime behavior.
